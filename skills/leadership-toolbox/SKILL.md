# Leadership Toolbox

Help the user understand the situation, choose a useful next action, prepare words to try and review what happened. This host-neutral bundle needs no paid model, connector, personal profile or running service. It contains 32 distinct public-source methods and original workplace applications.

## Route the request

For a direct method lookup, explanation or comparison, read the relevant [method-wiki entry](references/catalog.md) and full card, then answer directly with a practical limit. Follow the citation policy below. Do not impose an interview, invented baseline or review date.

For situational advice, default to the [adaptive interview](references/interview.md) within the [workflow](references/workflow.md); the user need not ask for probing. If a missing goal, concrete episode, stakes, authority, other account, prior attempt or capacity constraint could change the next move, ask the highest-value question first (one at a time preferred; never more than three focused questions in a turn), then wait for the answer. A brief provisional framing is fine; do not append a full recommendation, method shortlist or action plan before those answers. Reuse known facts and reflect corrections. Ask only while an answer could change the route, then recommend.

Answer directly when context is already sufficient or the user explicitly asks for a quick/provisional answer, skips questions or says stop. State decisive assumptions and limits; brevity alone does not mean skip needed probing. “Grill me” invites deeper challenge, not a prerequisite for any questions. Never demand disclosure or complete a questionnaire. Immediate protection takes precedence over waiting for answers.

Screen the proposed action for immediate harm, retaliation/coercion, privacy, authority, required expertise and method-specific prerequisites. Use the established qualified process for urgent danger, serious allegations and formal regulated decisions. A method or numerical descriptor cannot create authority, consent, skill or resources. Preparation does not authorise sending, assignment or system changes.

## Build a defensible match

Use [dimension anchors](references/dimensions.json) and [matching](references/matching.md), with the relevant entries in [structured catalog profiles](references/catalog.json). Keep factual time/constraints, categorical context, anchored consequence/reversibility, evidence provenance and unknowns separate. Do not sum scales, compute distance/success probabilities or score people. An absent account is unknown; a manager's account is not everyone's agreement.

Before narrowing a difficult or unfamiliar situation, read the relevant [knowledge topic](references/knowledge/README.md) to distinguish plausible mechanisms and rival explanations. Treat theory as a hypothesis to check, not a label to impose. Name a discriminating observation before selecting a remedy: what would you see if each explanation were true? Do not prespecify a blockage, skill deficit, unwillingness or agreement before it is observed. Choose a short candidate set by immediate job/output; read its full cards. Mark each eligible, conditional or excluded according to actual prerequisites. Compare the strongest two or three on job/output, context, feasibility, burden and the user's priorities. Explain one primary next move and its decisive condition. Show the strongest alternative when it would materially change the decision or the user asks for comparison. When a correction changes a decisive fact, update the profile and recommendation, not just the notes.

Use no intervention when no material problem warrants one. Use a sequence only when one method creates the next method's prerequisite, with an owner and transition condition. Sensemaking tools must lead to a useful decision, evidence step or action rather than only a diagram.

## Make it usable

After clarification is sufficient or the user explicitly requests provisional advice, give one next action, its reason, the decisive condition and useful words to try. Add only consequential unknowns; do not print the full profile, candidate table or method jargon by default. Respect the requested length; a one-line opening needs no citation or measurement appendix. Use the output/owner, later outcome, burden guardrail, review trigger and stop/switch contract when planning an actual trial or consequential change. For that trial, specify who would observe what at the next actual opportunity and by when; distinguish a proposed observer from an agreed one. Keep an unknown baseline explicit. If the cue or opportunity never occurs, record not tested, not failure, and choose a proportionate review/alternative cue rather than inventing an observation. Use one short operational definition, comparison and burden guardrail instead of vague improvement language. These details belong in an action plan, not an initial probing question or direct lookup. Use [measurement](references/measurement.md); distinguish output from outcome and prediction from observation. Never invent agreement, observed improvement or a scheduled review.

Use the [method evidence map](references/evidence-map.md) and the selected source's inspected-scope record before making an empirical claim. Distinguish the named procedure from the broader construct or intervention bundle studied; never present an abstract or book catalog as a fully read work. For ordinary practical advice and method explanations, use the original plain-language method wiki as the front door: normally at most one selected method's `guide_url` from [catalog.json](references/catalog.json), with a descriptive label. These are repository Markdown method pages, not a separate GitHub Wiki. No link is needed in an initial probing turn. Do not automatically send users to healthcare, health-and-safety or regulatory sites, or substitute a random encyclopedia page. Preserve source attribution and rights in the bundled cards.

For a research/evidence request or any evidence-sensitive claim (effect size, causality, effectiveness, clinical or regulatory assertion), cite the relevant underlying primary source and its actual study/inspection boundary; the wiki is not empirical proof. Healthcare evidence stays healthcare evidence and UK guidance stays jurisdiction-specific, not generic workplace validation. If no appropriate source was inspected, narrow or omit the claim. Follow [reference policy](references/reference-policy.md); do not claim to have read beyond the recorded scope. For specialist deferral, state the scope boundary without forcing a tangential citation. Use the appropriate process/qualified owner; do not adjudicate it with leadership methods.
