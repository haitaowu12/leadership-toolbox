## Shared ledger

Evidence: E1 — source/account, inspected material or user report, date if known, relevant scope/limit.
Node: N1 — role/activity/issue/constraint; short label; status; evidence basis or explicit assumption/unknown.
Relationship: R1 — N1 → N2; relationship verb and direction; status; evidence basis or unknown; authority/acceptance limit if relevant.
Unknown: U1 — unanswered question; which conclusion/edge/option depends on it; smallest useful evidence to seek.
Conflict: C1 — conflicting accounts with their evidence IDs; affected claim; dependent conclusion withheld.

Do not equate “reported” with independently observed or all parties' agreement. Use neutral role labels. Only add an arrow whose meaning can be stated; missing relationships stay unknown.

## Problem space, when useful

Issue P1 — observed/reported event or shortfall; source; desired outcome it concerns.
Hypothesis H1 — possible explanation; basis and rival H2; observation that could distinguish them.
Constraint K1 — reported/verified limit; who can change it, or unknown.

Keep hypothesis links visibly hypothetical; do not rank motivation, character or mental health. Dependencies and time order do not prove causes.
