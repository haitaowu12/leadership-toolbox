# Six everyday situations

These guides are original fictional leadership applications. They are not a course transcript or an additional leadership theory. Use only the section relevant to the request, then read the selected full method.

## G01 · Self and direction

When everything seems to depend on you, list the work that truly requires your authority or expertise, work that could transfer with support, and obligations that cannot simply disappear. Ask what contribution the team needs next. Use the [role review](../templates/role-review.md) to prepare an owner conversation. If the recipient is capable and available, [L01](methods/L01-delegation.md) makes a bounded agreement; if workload is the constraint, [L09](methods/L09-capacity.md) comes first. Do not treat a desired handoff as already accepted.

## G02 · Develop others

Start with a particular task. A demonstrated skill gap calls for [L12](methods/L12-teach-practice.md), including safe practice and observed performance. Someone with the skill who wants to explore options may benefit from [L02](methods/L02-grow.md). Unclear decision rights suggest [L01](methods/L01-delegation.md); missing time suggests [L09](methods/L09-capacity.md). Ask about access needs and support; silence or low confidence is not evidence of incompetence or unwillingness.

## G03 · Communication and trust

A specific episode and effect can be discussed using [L03](methods/L03-feedback.md), with inquiry into the other account. If the issue is a changed plan, use [L06](methods/L06-brief-huddle.md). When work moves between people, [L14](methods/L14-handoff.md) checks receipt and accepted ownership. Missing views may call for [L05](methods/L05-participation.md), including preparation, private input or a pass option. Serious allegations require the independent organisational route, protection from retaliation and realistic privacy boundaries; do not default to joint mediation.

## G04 · Change and influence

Identify who decides and who bears consequences. [L10](methods/L10-consultation.md) prepares consultation, evidence and a significant decision record. Use [L04](methods/L04-negotiation.md) for competing legitimate interests, and [L11](methods/L11-perspective.md) when different evidence or assumptions may explain disagreement. Expert disagreement on a consequential irreversible choice needs qualified examination, documented uncertainty and the legitimate owner. [L13](methods/L13-premortem.md) can surface risks before commitment; [L08](methods/L08-pdsa.md) is appropriate only when downside is bounded and reversal is feasible.

## G05 · Pressure and energy

Pause a heated reply long enough to distinguish the observed issue from the interpretation. Confirm what needs action now and what can wait. If demands exceed resources, use [L09](methods/L09-capacity.md) to make authorised tradeoffs instead of prescribing individual resilience. Active danger requires established containment, escalation and coverage first. After stabilisation, [L06](methods/L06-brief-huddle.md) can reset the plan. Personal health/clinical needs belong with qualified support; this guide does not assess fitness or diagnose stress.

## G06 · Reflection and team learning

For a completed episode, [L07](methods/L07-debrief.md) turns differing observations into one owned change. Distinguish operational learning from compelled emotional recounting after trauma. For a process hypothesis, [L08](methods/L08-pdsa.md) records a prediction and bounded test. Compare outcomes with burden, quality and downstream effects. “No change,” refusal, mixed results, abandoned actions and no intervention are legitimate records. Repeated local observations can inform the next choice but do not prove a universal effect.
