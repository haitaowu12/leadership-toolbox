# L14 · Closed-loop handoff

Version: 0.1.0 · Licence: MIT

**Job:** Transfer live work with checked receipt.

**Use when:** Work or an unresolved issue must move between people or shifts, with dependencies preserved.

**Avoid/stop when:** The recipient cannot accept responsibility, qualification or authority is absent, or critical details cannot be verified.

**Prerequisites:** An identified sender and recipient, current status and a clear acceptance/escalation boundary.

**Estimated time:** 5–15 minutes; use the established procedure in regulated work. This is an editorial planning estimate.

**Participants/preparation:** A leader and the relevant person(s); use one concrete task or episode, relevant evidence and the stated prerequisites. Invite access needs and a way to raise concerns privately. Facilitation is required for group input; qualification remains with the task owner.

## Try it

1. State the current result/status and what remains outstanding.
2. Explain the next action, constraints, risk signs and whom to contact.
3. Ask the recipient to restate the critical action and clarify gaps.
4. Obtain explicit acceptance within their remit, or keep/escalate ownership until an appropriate recipient accepts.

**Words to try:** “What is your next action, and can you accept this responsibility with the available resources?”

**Immediate output:** A received handoff with current owner, next action, risks and confirmation.

**One outcome signal:** At the next checkpoint, did the recipient take the intended action without losing a critical dependency? Agree the definition, baseline (or unknown), observation window and review date before acting.

**Guardrail and stop/switch:** Watch silent assumptions of acceptance or a transfer that strands unresolved risk. Do not mark ownership transferred merely because a message was sent.

## Fictional example

A recurring support task moves to another colleague. Confirm the open issue, next action and escalation contact before treating the transfer as complete.
