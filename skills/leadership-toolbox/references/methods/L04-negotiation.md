# L04 · Interest-based negotiation

Version: 0.1.0 · Licence: MIT

**Job:** Resolve incompatible demands.

**Use when:** Parties have different requests but can explore underlying needs and options.

**Avoid/stop when:** You lack authority to make the commitment, the process is coercive, or safety/legal rights are being bargained away.

**Prerequisites:** A decision owner and clarity about commitments that can actually change.

**Estimated time:** 20–45 minutes; more for complex disputes. This is an editorial planning estimate.

**Participants/preparation:** A leader and the relevant person(s); use one concrete task or episode, relevant evidence and the stated prerequisites. Invite access needs and a way to raise concerns privately. Facilitation is required for group input; qualification remains with the task owner.

## Try it

1. State each requested position and ask why it matters.
2. Separate constraints from preferences.
3. Generate options that meet more than one interest.
4. Agree fair criteria and compare options.
5. Take the proposal to the authorised owner when agreement exceeds your remit.

**Words to try:** “What need does that deadline protect, and what alternative could meet it?”

**Immediate output:** Options, criteria and an authorised decision or escalation request.

**One outcome signal:** By the review date, have incompatible commitments been reconciled and understood by affected people? Agree the definition, baseline (or unknown), observation window and review date before acting.

**Guardrail and stop/switch:** Watch hidden losers, unconsulted dependencies or a promise beyond your authority. Escalate if the conflict cannot be resolved within the available remit.
