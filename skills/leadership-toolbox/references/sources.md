## Additional evidence and practical boundaries

- [GROW provider terms](https://www.performanceconsultants.com/terms-and-conditions/) and [CCL republication policy](https://www.ccl.org/policies/reprint-permission/): do not reproduce protected provider expression or assets.
- [PON agreement trap](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/batna/too-eager-to-close-nb/): agreement itself is not the success criterion; compare realistic alternatives and absent stakeholders’ costs.
- [Liberating Structures licence](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/cc-license): current pages state CC BY-SA 4.0; pre-June-2026 material may have different terms. No older asset is distributed. L05’s licence/attribution is also recorded in its file.
- [AHRQ huddle](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/team/tools/huddle.html) and [teach-back](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/teachback.html): checking understanding is distinct from observing skill. [Toolkit public-domain notes](https://www.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/wysiwyg/teamstepps-program/dx-improvement/module1-presenters-notes.pdf) do not establish rights to every separately credited asset or imply endorsement.
- [Tannenbaum and Cerasoli debrief meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23516804/): findings concern practices under studied conditions; no promised percentage gain is made here.
- [Taylor and colleagues’ PDSA review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3963536/): inconsistent application/reporting; no pooled guarantee for this card. [IHI Model for Improvement and permissions](https://www.ihi.org/library/model-for-improvement) does not give blanket permission to distribute its figure.
- [HSE management standards](https://www.hse.gov.uk/stress/standards/overview.htm): attend to work design, demands, control and support. Do not generalise UK legal statements to other jurisdictions. L09 uses original planning guidance and copies no HSE assets.
- [Kanban Guide](https://kanbanguides.org/the-kanban-guide/): flow concepts can inform work review, but current same-page licence statements conflict. No Kanban assets are reused and L09 does not claim a complete Kanban implementation.
- [Edmondson’s 1999 study](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/13a7b031-0fdd-45ec-a7e0-2b80e2bc679f) and [Frazier and colleagues’ synthesis](https://digitalcommons.odu.edu/management_fac_pubs/13/): evidence about psychological safety is not proof that a short script causes performance gains. No staff safety rating is created here.
- [NICE PTSD guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG116/chapter/recommendations): psychologically focused debriefing to prevent/treat PTSD is distinct from ordinary operational learning. Do not compel trauma recounting.
- [Nygard’s decision records](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions) is a software architecture experience report. [NASA decision analysis](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-8-decision-analysis/) supports documented alternatives/rationale in high-consequence technical settings. The general-work decision template is an editorial application, not a proven intervention.
- [Copyright Office on methods and expression](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf): public ideas/methods and protected expression are different. Original commentary does not license third-party articles, figures or trademarks.

[Framework comparison](framework-choices.md) holds Cynefin/situational-leadership sources. Source review informs this release; publishers may later change pages/terms. Before adding any new copied material, examine that specific asset and preserve its actual licence/attribution. Do not call all source material cleared.
