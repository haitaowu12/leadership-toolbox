## Read source roles correctly

- **Original empirical study:** inspect setting, design, comparison, outcomes, attrition, alternative explanations and applicability. A field association is not automatically causal.
- **Systematic review/meta-analysis:** inspect inclusion dates, selection/quality, heterogeneity, study dependence, publication bias and which outcomes were actually pooled. It may concern a broad family rather than a named script.
- **Theory/research synthesis or scholarly chapter:** use it to explain mechanisms and competing interpretations; distinguish proposed mechanisms from observed mediation or tested interventions.
- **Textbook/book preview:** learn concepts at the recorded edition and scope. A sample is not the entire book; a publisher/catalog record alone supports only identification and a reading route.
- **Originator/institutional guidance:** often best for procedure and provenance. It cannot by itself establish efficacy, and institutional hosting alone is not a quality guarantee.

These roles can coexist and are not an ordered evidence ladder. Judge a source against the claim it is being used to support. Count, prestige, open access and study design alone do not supply a global score.

## Knowledge acquisition and reuse policy

The delivered knowledge is independently written explanation, concise research notes, metadata and links. No copyrighted full books/articles, provider graphics, questionnaire item banks or source worksheets are ingested into the distributed package. Public accessibility is not public domain or permission to redistribute. Open licences can include noncommercial, no-derivatives and share-alike conditions; separately credited assets may differ. Current OpenStax pages also display additional AI-use notices; they are linked reading, not raw ingestion assets. These statements record source notices and conservative project practice, not legal clearance.

Source numbers, statistical effects and population contexts are retained in notes to permit scrutiny, not to predict this user's result. Negative/null findings and limitations remain beside favorable findings. Specific procedural additions are our editorial applications. Neither this library nor its source set validates the matching dimensions, advice scripts or human effectiveness of the whole toolbox.

## Maintain the library

When revising a claim, preserve the stable ID, add the actual version/access/scope, and check for corrections or more recent syntheses. Update both [source records](sources.json) and [method mapping](evidence-map.json), then the relevant knowledge and card. Record what changed in procedure, question or observation, rather than just adding a citation. A gap remains explicit until evidence actually answers it. This was a targeted expansion, not an exhaustive systematic search; no fixed source count is a completion criterion.
