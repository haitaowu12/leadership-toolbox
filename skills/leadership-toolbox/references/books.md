# Books and longer readings: edition and inspection register

These records distinguish substantial inspected chapters, a public publisher excerpt, and an unread book identified by metadata. No complete copyrighted book is bundled, and whole-book reading is not inferred from availability. Read the [topic explanations](knowledge/README.md) before diving into a long source. The structured record adds reuse and claim limits.
