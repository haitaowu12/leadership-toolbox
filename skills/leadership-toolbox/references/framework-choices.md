# Why this front door is simple

| Approach | Useful contribution | Limit in this toolbox |
|---|---|---|
| Goal and constraints routing | Focuses on the next observable change, decision owner and workable conditions | Original editorial navigation; not an empirically validated leadership theory |
| Explicit decision branches | Makes safety, authority, capacity and escalation boundaries visible | Navigation cannot prove a human diagnosis; preserve unknowns and allow revision |
| Cynefin | Helps question whether a familiar response fits clear, complicated, complex or chaotic contexts; mixed contexts are possible | Optional sense-making framework rather than a mandatory method, person label or complexity score |
| Situational leadership | Encourages attention to task-specific direction, support and autonomy | Research is mixed/conditional; do not import proprietary diagnostics or employee maturity categories |

Use the [short workflow](workflow.md) for daily practice. Add Cynefin when context distinctions clarify why expert analysis, a reversible probe or stabilisation is appropriate. Do not call a risky irreversible rollout a probe merely because its outcome is uncertain. Do not force a domain label when several components differ or evidence is missing.

Cynefin originator explanations distinguish frameworks from methods and categorisation from sense-making: [domains](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin_Domains), [origin discussion](https://thecynefin.co/part-five-origins-of-cynefin/). This file is original comparative commentary; no diagram, wiki text or training asset is reproduced. Cynefin is a registered trademark of its owner; there is no affiliation or endorsement.

Situational leadership evidence includes a [2009 test of three versions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1048984309001507) and [2018 conditional-support study](https://doi.org/10.1108/LODJ-01-2018-0050). These do not justify conclusive blanket confirmation or dismissal. This package uses observable task needs without reproducing the proprietary assessments.
