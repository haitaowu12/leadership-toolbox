# Feedback that helps the next decision

Choose observations with the people affected. Use plain definitions and modest collection effort. Never convert these fields into a person score, success probability or cross-method ranking.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Immediate output | What the action produced: accepted handoff, explicit tradeoff, observed practice, decision or unresolved concern |
| Later outcome signal | One desired change; definition, baseline or “unknown,” observation window, source/observer and review date |
| Balancing guardrail | One cost or harm that could rise: hidden overtime, support load, quality loss, excluded input or displaced work; locally agreed stop threshold |
| Review decision | Retain, adapt, switch, stop, no intervention or gather evidence, with the reason and owner |

Before action, write a prediction as a claim to test, not a percentage: “After the plan reset, each affected person can explain their next action at Friday’s checkpoint.” If the baseline is unavailable, say so. A completed conversation is an output; later task change is a different observation. An observed before/after difference can have other causes.

For counts, define the denominator and period. For timing, define start/end events. For ratings, state the question and whose subjective account it is. Disagreement is evidence to retain, not a score to average away. Avoid surveillance, collecting sensitive detail or mandatory surveys for trivial actions. A small qualitative check is often enough.

**Example (fictional):** A trial reduces meeting length from a locally observed 40 minutes to 25 across two meetings, but support preparation rises from 10 to 35 minutes. The output is a shorter meeting; the burden guardrail suggests adapting or stopping. Two observations do not establish causality or justify automatic scale-up.

Card signals are prompts to define locally. Their wording and thresholds are editorial, not validated instruments. Read [sources](sources.md) for what the source actually supports. [Practice template](../templates/practice-record.md) keeps prediction, observation and decision separate.
