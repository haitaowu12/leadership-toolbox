# What each method can claim

Every card is an editorial workplace rendition. A linked study may concern a wider construct, an intervention family or a multicomponent bundle. Below, no research found means not established in this purposeful review, not proof that no evidence exists. A book's metadata-only entry is further reading, never content evidence. See [source records](sources.json) for design/access scope and [knowledge topics](knowledge/README.md) for mechanisms.

## L01

**Outcome delegation agreement.** [Card](methods/L01-delegation.md) · [Knowledge](knowledge/coaching-development.md).

- Provenance: [P01](provenance.md#p01)
- Research: [E02](knowledge/coaching-development.md#e02), [E03](knowledge/coaching-development.md#e03), [E06](knowledge/coaching-development.md#e06)
- Book/chapters: [B60](knowledge/inquiry-coordination.md#b60)
- **Supported scope:** Autonomy-support and work-redesign research informs conditions; no direct test of this agreement.
- **Limit:** Decision rights, useful support and actual work conditions must change; fewer questions alone is not improvement.
- **Open question:** Which delegation conditions improve delivery without suppressing necessary escalation in this setting?
