Scope: all 34 unique external URLs in the original portion of [sources.md](https://github.com/haitaowu12/leadership-toolbox/blob/9f49b19f7cf2b4b0bda7505e43e7b52a5a3d867d/skills/leadership-toolbox/references/sources.md) (before “Expansion sources”) and [framework-choices.md](https://github.com/haitaowu12/leadership-toolbox/blob/9f49b19f7cf2b4b0bda7505e43e7b52a5a3d867d/skills/leadership-toolbox/references/framework-choices.md). ## Result

All 34 original URLs were attempted: 28 accessible/supported through direct retrieval; 6 reader-blocked or reader-failed, with their bounded claims corroborated through official indexed content or institutional records; 0 confirmed stale/broken. “Reader-blocked” describes the research reader's access, not whether a person can open the page. No denial, CAPTCHA, login or paywall was bypassed.

“Supported” means the retrieved page supports the narrow provenance, practice, study-summary or reuse-notice statement recorded below. It does not establish that a toolbox card, script, matching rule or situation scale is validated, or that all source material is cleared for redistribution. Academic abstract/metadata checks are identified; this is not a full-paper methodological review, legal opinion or asset-by-asset rights clearance.

## Recommended release wording

No confirmed dead original URL requires removal. The most useful changes are a more direct Huddle citation for L06's reset claim and a narrow notice such as:

> Performance Consultants' current GROW page and terms assert licensing, reuse and AI-processing restrictions for its original model/materials. This toolbox uses independently authored commentary and does not reproduce its graphics, instruments or templates. This source review records the provider's notice; it does not determine enforceability or provide legal clearance.

Keep the existing limitations on field validation, sector transfer, proprietary instruments, old Liberating Structures assets and conflicting Kanban notices. Optional accessibility supplements are the Cornell record for L10 and PubMed record for L11. Retain original DOIs for stable identification. Access conditions and publisher notices may change after this date.
